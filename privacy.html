Privacy policy: Chrome extensions by Frank Gilroy.

Last updated 2026-11-13.

These extensions do their work inside your browser. They read the page you are on only when you click the extension, and only to do the job described in each listing. Page content, browsing history and form data are not sent anywhere.

Settings are stored with Chrome's storage on your device (and in your Chrome sync storage if you use Chrome sync).

Payments. If you buy Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe (stripe.com). They receive your email address and payment details to process the purchase and to let you restore Pro on another browser. I receive your email address, the plan and the payment status. I don't receive card numbers. The extension asks ExtensionPay whether your install has Pro; it sends a random install identifier, not page data.

No analytics, no ads, no sale of data. Data is not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, not used for creditworthiness or lending, and not sold to data brokers. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions or deletion requests: frankjgilroy@gmail.com.
