Fake Form Filler – Test Data Autofill

Fill every field of a web form with realistic fake data in one click. Works from the toolbar icon or right-click menu.

Description

Complete a sign-up, checkout or contact page with believable sample data in one click. Made for developers, QA and designers who are tired of typing "asdf" into every field.

How to use
1. Open the page with the form you want to try.
2. Click the extension icon, or right-click the page and choose "Fill form with fake data".
3. Review the result and submit.

Features
- Names, emails (always on example.com), phone numbers, addresses, dates, numbers and text
- Dropdowns, radio groups and checkboxes are chosen for you
- Works with pages built on modern frameworks
- Payment, identity and security-code fields are always left empty

Privacy: the extension works on the page you click it on; nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere.

Support: frankjgilroy@gmail.com

Permissions

  • activeTab: fill the form on the current page only after the user clicks the icon or the context-menu item.
  • scripting: run the small script that writes values into the form fields.
  • storage: keep the saved profile, the count of free Pro tries and a cached Pro status.
  • contextMenus: add the "Fill form with fake data" right-click item.
