Form testing checklist: what to type into a form

A form that works with "test" and "test@test.com" can still break on real people. Use this list as a starting set, and tick it off per form.

Names

  • A long surname and a long given name (40+ characters).
  • An apostrophe or hyphen: O'Brien, Smith-Jones.
  • Accents and non-Latin letters: José, Zoë, Łukasz, 山田.
  • A single-word name, and a name with a space at the end.

Email

  • A plus tag: name+test@example.com.
  • Uppercase letters, and a subdomain.
  • A missing @, a missing dot, and two @ signs, which should be refused.

Use the reserved example.com domain for test mail so a stray send reaches nobody.

Phone and address

  • International format with a plus sign, spaces and dashes.
  • Too few and too many digits.
  • Address line two empty, a very long street name, a postcode with a leading zero.

Dates and numbers

  • 29 February in a leap year and in a non-leap year.
  • Today, yesterday and a date far in the future.
  • Zero, a negative number, a decimal with a comma and with a point.

Text areas

  • Empty, one character, and 5,000 characters.
  • Line breaks, emoji and angle brackets such as <b>, to check output escaping.

Behaviour

  • Submit twice quickly, to catch duplicate records.
  • Press Back after submitting, then submit again.
  • Leave each required field empty one at a time.
