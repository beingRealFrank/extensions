Test email addresses for sign-up forms that reach nobody

Testing a sign-up form with a made-up address can send mail to a stranger. Use addresses that are safe by design.

Reserved domains

The domains example.com, example.org and example.net are reserved for documentation and testing. Mail sent to them goes nowhere, so a stray send is harmless. The .test top-level domain is reserved too.

Plus addressing for your own inbox

If you need the mail to arrive, use your own address with a tag: you+signup1@yourdomain.com. Most providers deliver it to you@yourdomain.com, and each tag counts as a different address for uniqueness checks. Some forms reject the plus sign, which is itself a bug worth reporting.

What to avoid

  • Real people's addresses.
  • Guessed company addresses.
  • Throwaway-mail sites for anything that holds real data.
